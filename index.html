Digital Thorium

डिजिटल थोरियम
A market architecture for India's thorium century. The convergence of reserves, compute, and ownership.

This is a proposal to create a tradable, fractional, on-chain claim on a unit of thorium-derived electricity produced by a federally supervised reactor fleet. The unit of account is one kilowatt-hour-year. Issuance is federal, through a new authority sitting between the Department of Atomic Energy and the Securities and Exchange Board of India.

The site below accompanies a five-document policy package. It contains an interactive model parameterised on the central case from the report, an illustrative view of the holder account, and links to the underlying documents.

Notes

Digital Thorium is a policy proposal. The instrument has not been issued, and any issuance would require Parliament, with implementing regulations from the Securities and Exchange Board of India and the Reserve Bank of India. Final terms would be set in cohort-specific prospectuses.

Figures shown in the simulator and the holder view are illustrative, drawn from the central case in the policy report and term sheet. Comparison rates for fixed deposits, the Nifty 50, and gold are long-run nominal averages rather than guarantees. Outcomes depend on reactor commissioning, the trajectory of compute-driven electricity demand, and the durability of the statutory framework. Each can disappoint.

Nothing on this site constitutes investment advice or an offer of securities.
